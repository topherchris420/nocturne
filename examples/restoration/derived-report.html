What uncertainty costs

64 input values are ranges. Every target is checked at the pessimistic end of every range; the shaded band on each bar spans the optimistic to pessimistic result.

On central values the cheapest plan would cost $4,550.00. Planning for the pessimistic case costs $400.00 more: that is what better measurement of the largest remaining terms could save.

Where effects do not simply add

Declared interactions between options, such as a rebound when one owner dims or a shared reflecting surface, are charged to the plans that trigger them.

  • In effect: Merchants leave security floodlights on after the sign curfewINVENTED rebound; a source-isolation trial with the floodlights logged would measure it.

Where the coefficients came from

nocturne derive: single-scattering zenith luminance (vdl7.skyglow kernels), photopic SQM-like band. Calibration factor: [0.8, 1.0, 1.25].

  • Single scattering in a plane-parallel atmosphere: no multiple scattering or Earth curvature (significant beyond about 30 km), no terrain or building obstruction.
  • Direct uplight is emitted evenly over the upper hemisphere and reflected light as a Lambertian surface; real luminaires and facades have their own distributions.
  • Absolute contributions carry the calibration factor; before/after ratios are far less sensitive to it. Calibrate against measured zenith brightness before relying on absolute values.
  • Lumens are those emitted during the assessed window; a curfew or booking schedule enters as an output factor.

What the planner could not use

  • crossing / dim: below declared service floor
  • monument / curfew: owner authorization missing
  • offices / all-off: below declared service floor

Prove the change, then plan again

Preregister randomized baseline/dim windows for one consenting source group at a time. Compare a protected receptor with an unaffected control station. Retain failed windows, estimate the effect in linear luminance, and update only the source coefficients supported by those observations.

Limits of this result

  • Planning result only. No commands are sent to lights.
  • Every result is conditional on the supplied contributions and complete action packages.
  • Contributions add exactly within one scenario (radiative transfer is linear in its sources); the approximations are the scenario set, the supplied coefficients and the declared interactions.
  • Scenarios are stress cases, not statistical confidence intervals; ranges are checked at their pessimistic end, not given probabilities.
  • Service fractions and review flags are declarations, not a photometric safety certification.
  • Artificial zenith luminance is not a measure of glare, window trespass, or habitat illuminance.
  • Unmanaged light remains in every portfolio; no energy savings or star counts are inferred.
