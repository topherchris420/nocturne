Start with your own light.
Close existing blinds while keeping occupied rooms lit. Switch off only a separately controlled, unnecessary circuit after its last use. Check at the start of the window; if people or required service need it, leave it on and record the exception.
A building’s closing checklist can become a daily light check. Neighbours can coordinate their windows and closing times without waiting for a citywide controller.
Electricity estimates are not skyglow measurements. Window-group hours don’t describe area or light intensity. Phone photos can document spill, but automatic exposure cannot establish a calibrated change in sky brightness.


Use DC’s existing routes.
  • DC 311: submit your streetlight observation and keep the reference number.
  • DDOT response guidance: investigations/simple maintenance are listed at 2–5 days; upgrades including lights that are too bright can take up to 12 months.
  • Lights Out DC: join the existing campaign.
  • Cornell BirdCast guidance: reduce nonessential light and window spill from dusk to dawn during migration.
Sources checked October 15, 2026. A report can start today; a repair is not promised tonight. Federal properties need their own responsible operator.
