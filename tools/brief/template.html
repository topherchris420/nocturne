Nocturne · a proposal for the District of Columbia · October 2026

Nocturne for Washington

DC already owns the hard part. Its 72,512 streetlights are 97 % LED, and 96 % sit on a network that can dim them. A night schedule pushed to those nodes saves about 5.1 GWh a year with no new hardware. Roll it out as a randomised trial with a safety brake, measure it at the sky, then bring the rest of the city's lighting onto the same curfew.

What people will see when they look up

DC's 550 m light grid, carried through the atmosphere to six places where people go to see the night. Streetlights are only part of the glow. Rollout

A randomised trial with a brake, every ward in every wave

DC's 12,106 lit street segments are split into four waves by ward and road class. The draw is seeded from a text published in advance, so anyone can re-run it. Before each wave, night-time crashes on switched segments are compared with segments not yet switched over the same nights. A signal pauses the next wave and reverts that class.

Illustrative draw; the real seed is published before the draw. DC recorded 18,173 crashes in 2025. Roughly half happened after dark, on the order of 0.002 per lit segment-night. Detecting a 20 % change takes about 190,000–240,000 segment-nights per arm. The wedge has that by the end of its second wave.

This quarter

What DDOT decides

  1. Adopt the class schedule and the floors as policy. Each class has five levels: evening, 22:00, curfew, deep night and dawn. No street goes below its RP-8 class, no High-Injury Network segment below 60 %, and no unsensed street below the Phase 0 floor.
  2. Confirm who may change schedules under the streetlight programme's contract, and how energy savings are counted and shared.
  3. Publish the seed text, then draw the waves. Name the brake's data feed: night-time crashes by location from the District's crash layer.
  4. Load wave 1 into the central system, and open a 311 category for "street too dark at night" that routes to an operator override for that segment.
  5. Buy six sky-quality meters, one for each place above. A season of readings measures the curfew step and calibrates the sky model with DC's own numbers.
  6. Invite the other lighting owners onto the Night Signal: NPS, GSA, the Architect of the Capitol and the Smithsonian, business improvement districts, stadiums and sign owners. Start with heavy-migration nights alongside Lights Out DC.
Evidence

What the research says, including where it cuts against us

Dimming has not cost safety

Across 62 local authorities in England and Wales, switch-off, part-night lighting, dimming and white light showed little evidence of harm to night-time collisions or crime (Steinbach et al. 2015).

Light where people are does help

In a randomised trial in New York public housing, added lighting cut night-time outdoor index crime by at least 36 % (Chalfin et al. 2022). Nocturne brings the light back within two seconds when someone is there.

Streetlights are a minority of the glow

In Tucson, about 14 % of the emissions behind skyglow came from city streetlights (Barentine et al. 2020), and 13 % of the light seen from orbit after midnight (Kyba et al. 2021). That is why the Night Signal exists.

The sky is getting brighter, fast

Citizen observations show the sky brightening 7–10 % a year from 2011 to 2022 (Kyba et al. 2023). The Milky Way is already hidden from nearly 80 % of North Americans (Falchi et al. 2016).

Sleep, birds and insects

The sleep-environment guide is at most 1 lx melanopic EDI (Brown et al. 2022). Migrating birds caught in a beam dispersed within minutes once it went dark (Van Doren et al. 2017). Light at night is a driver of insect decline (Owens et al. 2020).

How these numbers were made. The fleet is DDOT's streetlight asset layer. Crashes come from the District's crash layer. Energy is a year of nights on each node's own astronomical clock and DST rule. The sky is a single-scattering model, molecules plus aerosol, over DC's 550 m light grid. It is calibrated to Tucson's measured streetlight share until DC has its own meters.

Assumed: the energy price and grid factor (both set above), aerosol load, ground reflectance, luminaire efficacy and the post-top globes' uplight. The baseline assumes lights run at their commissioned maximum all night. A dimming schedule already in place would shrink the Phase 0 saving.
