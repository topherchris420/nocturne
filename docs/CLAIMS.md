# Nocturne's evidence ladder

Each claim must match the type of evidence behind it. A computed result is not a completed intervention, and a recorded switch event is not a measured change in the sky.

| Label | What it means | What it does **not** mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Design** | A documented proposal, protocol, controller interface, or planning assumption | Owner consent, operational support or real-world effect |
| **Simulation** | Software result against declared inputs or invented examples; reproducible with the stated version | Calibrated physics or observed DC outcomes |
| **Verified software** | Automated checks pass for the tested version and environment | Physical hardware, photometric, legal or operational approval |
| **Owner reported** | An authorized person records completion of a specific action against a matched plan | Independently confirmed energy or sky brightness |
| **Field measured** | Calibrated observations, time-aligned controls, exclusions and a linked protocol demonstrate an effect at a named receptor | Proof of generality, long-term safety or whole-city improvement |
| **Operator deployed** | A responsible system operator confirms the real installation, monitoring and rollback | Automatically sustained outcomes or citywide impact |

## Claims currently supported

- The planner searches complete combinations of declared interventions and reports feasibility conditional on its model.
- DC Tonight generates human action sheets and reconciles self-reported completions, with no live actuation.
- The example source-isolation study recovers values *in generated data*, not from DC measurements.
- DDOT-derived lighting aggregates inform a **modeled** business case whose maximum-output baseline and prices may not match current billing or schedules.
- A GitHub Actions workflow tests software when it runs. A green workflow is evidence of checks passing, not certification.

## Claims **not yet** supported

- Nocturne has reduced Washington, DC's skyglow.
- Its energy savings estimate is a measured saving on a real electric bill.
- All registered DDOT nodes can execute its schedules.
- A lighting reduction meets applicable road/pedestrian photometric requirements without an engineer's review.
- Its simulated star counts represent a guaranteed field outcome.

When the first pilot is measured, include a public, privacy-safe protocol fingerprint, immutable analysis artifact, instrument/calibration record, independent control, raw-data availability statement and explicit limitations. Use [FIELD_PILOT.md](FIELD_PILOT.md) rather than converting a synthetic result into a field claim.

**Rule:** If measured evidence is missing, write *modeled*, *estimated*, *proposed*, *self-reported* or *unknown*, as appropriate.
